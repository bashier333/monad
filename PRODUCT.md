# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Small lenders and advances teams deciding fast on small-business borrowers: fund, review, or kill.

## Product Purpose

Monad turns public open data into sourced risk flags in about 40 seconds so small lenders skip dead borrowers. Success means faster decisions with fewer write-offs.

## Positioning

Open decision, delegated to builder. Candidate mechanisms from the current build: public-only fusion with every flag cited to its source row, replay backtest showing kill signals ahead of failures, and no core or ledger integration.

## Operating Context

Pre-funding review: type a company with state or domain, read liens plus suits plus shutdown signals, then decide. App-only model: checks run through the Engine backend at `app/api/check/route.ts`; the marketing frontpage lives at `app/page.tsx` (platform page at `app/platforms/ontology/page.tsx`) and offers no free web tier. Desktop exe opens to an ontology workspace (`app/workspace/page.tsx`); the twin renders a force-graph network and a site map above the lists.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Public sources only: registry, courts, liens, reviews, hiring, news, filings, shipping, domains. Informational signal only; the lender decides and credit-rule counsel stays outside the product. No invented testimonials, customers, or backtest claims.

## Brand Commitments

Name: Monad. Binding visual constraint volunteered by the user: sleek minimalistic UI. Recorded without expansion.

## Evidence on Hand

No real testimonials, customer logos, or measured backtest dataset on file. Site copy is sample-free. Future work must not fabricate proof.

## Product Principles

1. Sources over scores: every call cites the row it came from.
2. Public only: nothing behind logins, nothing touching money movement.
3. Lender decides: the product flags, the human funds.
4. Speed with receipts: seconds to an answer, links to verify it.
5. No fake proof: sample stays labeled sample until real evidence exists.
