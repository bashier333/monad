# No-show playbook (P-256)

- 5 min late: message with the screen-share link + "no worries, ready when you are".
- 15 min late: cancel graciously, propose 2 specific slots (never "let me know").
- Second no-show: ask if the problem is timing or priority — believe the answer.
- Never guilt. A pilot who won't meet won't convert; reallocate to pipeline.
