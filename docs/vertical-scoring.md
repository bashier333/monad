# Vertical scoring rubric (X10 E-451, v0 estimates 2026-09-18)

## Rubric

Score 1–5 each: pain $ (does the mess cost real money weekly?) × reachability
(can we DM the buyer without warm intros?) × data-mess (CSV exports exist?) ×
pay-speed (will they pay in 30 days?). Multiply; rank.

## Kill-score (E-458)

A vertical is rejected when: no CSV export exists (data-mess = 1), buyer needs
procurement (pay-speed = 1), or two pilots fail the kill-gate. Rejections stay
filed below with the reason — re-score quarterly, numbers change as we learn.
