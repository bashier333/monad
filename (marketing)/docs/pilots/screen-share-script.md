# Screen-share script (P-251) — they drive, you narrate

"You share your screen. I'll talk, you click. Stop me the second anything is confusing —
confusion is the product telling us what to fix."

1. "Open your TMS export menu — where would you pull last week's settlements?"
2. "Drag that file here. Watch what it detects — correct anything it gets wrong out loud."
3. "Here's your week. Find your worst lane. Does the number match your gut?"
4. "Click it. Every dollar links to its source row — chase one you disagree with."
5. "Flag it. Tell me where it belongs. Watch next week's answer change."
