# Culture + hiring notes (R-921/R-923/R-993/R-994/R-995/R-997)

## Hiring tied to triggers (R-921)

Triggers from `docs/hiring-triggers.md` rule — not dates, not vibes. Structured
interviews with scorecards (R-922 note: scorecard template lives with the role doc).

## Onboarding 30/60/90 (R-923)

Day 1: suite + seed + messy fixture. Day 30: shipped a preset. Day 60: owned a
pack area. Day 90: led a retro. Buddy assigned week one.

## Writing culture (R-993)

Memos over meetings. RFCs for big changes, ADRs for decisions, kill-list for honesty.

## Customer closeness (R-994)

Everyone does support monthly — engineers answer real tickets, founders join calls.

## Shipping celebrations (R-995)

Launches and kills both count. Retro published whatever the outcome.

## Remote-first (R-997)

Async default, overlap hours posted, decisions in writing. Offsites twice yearly
when the team exists.
